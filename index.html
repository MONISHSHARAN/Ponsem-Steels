Understanding Grades

What the numbers on steel mean

TMT grades in India follow IS 1786. The number is the minimum yield strength in N/mm², and the letters tell you about ductility and corrosion resistance.

500D

The everyday standard

Minimum yield strength of 500 N/mm² with "D" ductility. Widely used for residential construction.

Yield strength500 N/mm²
550D

Stronger, still ductile

Minimum 550 N/mm². Higher strength with the "D" ductility that matters in seismic zones. This is the NADI 550D grade.

Yield strength550 N/mm²
600CRS

Strength + corrosion shield

Minimum 600 N/mm², with CRS alloying that slows rust. Ideal near the sea and in wet conditions. This is the MS LIFE 600+ CRS grade.

Yield strength600 N/mm²

"D" stands for higher ductility. Under IS 1786, D-grade bars must meet stricter requirements for elongation and the ratio of tensile to yield strength. That means they stretch and bend more before failing, which is why they're preferred in earthquake-prone areas.

CRS means Corrosion Resistant Steel. Small alloying additions slow down rusting of the bar inside concrete. It's recommended for coastal regions, high-humidity areas, basements, sumps, water tanks and any structure regularly exposed to moisture. MS LIFE 600+ CRS is our choice for these conditions.

Genuine branded TMT bars carry the brand name and grade rolled or embossed along the bar, plus the ISI mark. For VIZAG, for example, look for the embossed "VIZAG" marking. Always buy from a dealer you trust, and ask for a proper invoice.

Steel prices follow raw-material costs and market demand, so rates can move daily. That's why we quote on the day. Message us on WhatsApp for the current price for your brand, size and quantity.

TMT bars are commonly used in 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 25 and 32 mm diameters. Availability depends on the brand and grade, so tell us your bar schedule and we'll confirm what's in stock.
