About Ponsem Steels

Every great home begins with strong steel.

Ponsem Steels is built on one simple promise: give every customer genuine, high-grade products at a fair price, delivered when they need them. We started with the steel that goes into your foundations, columns and slabs, and now supply the door that welcomes people into the finished home.

Our TMT division deals in NADI 550D, MS LIFE 600+ CRS and VIZAG reinforcement bars. Our Steel Doors division offers 28 designs across GI steel, cast iron, aluminium and smart-lock series. One partner, honest advice and the right product at every stage.

Mission

Make quality steel products simple to buy, with transparent pricing and dependable supply.

Vision

Values
