Inside a Metal Door

Beauty on the outside. Strength within.

A typical metal security door is built in layers, each with a job. Hover a layer to highlight it. Ask us for the exact specification of any model.

1
Designer faceThe finish you see: wood-grain, cast relief or smooth modern panels.
2
Metal skinSteel, cast iron or aluminium sheet that resists impact and weather.
3
Filled coreAn infill that adds rigidity and helps with sound and heat.
4
Steel frameA rigid frame and hinges that hold everything square for years.
Why Metal Doors

Built for Indian weather & life

Wooden doors are beautiful, but they need constant care. Metal doors give you the look with far less worry.

Steel & metal doors Our pick

  • Termites can’t eat steel
  • No swelling, shrinking or warping in the monsoon
  • Galvanised (zinc-coated) steel resists rust
  • Low upkeep: wipe clean, occasional touch-up
  • Strong, secure feel with a solid frame
  • Wood-grain, cast and modern finishes available

Traditional wood

  • Needs termite treatment and regular checks
  • Can swell, crack or warp with humidity
  • Regular polishing and repainting
  • Quality hardwood is increasingly expensive
  • Natural warmth, if you’re ready to maintain it

Choosing your door

Still deciding? Send us a photo of your entrance and we’ll suggest designs.

Ask on WhatsApp

For everyday main, back or utility doors, the FM Series (GI steel) is the practical choice. For a grand villa entrance, look at the Premium Series (cast iron). For bold designs in rust-free metal, the Iconic Series (aluminium). For keyless convenience, the Smart Series.

Sizes are height × width in millimetres. For example, 2050 × 1080 is about 6′9″ × 3′6″. Some sizes show variant codes (SL/ML), which we explain when we quote. Iconic and Smart Series doors are made to your exact size. We always confirm with an on-site measurement.

It’s a double door with one large leaf for daily use and a smaller leaf that opens only when you need extra width, for example to move furniture. You get a grand look with an everyday-sized opening.

Metal doors don’t swell or warp like wood. Galvanised steel has a zinc coating that resists rust, and aluminium doesn’t rust. Near the sea, a good finish and simple upkeep keep any metal door looking its best. Ask us for the right option for your location.
